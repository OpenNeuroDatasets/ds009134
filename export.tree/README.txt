We studied the interaction of the neural dynamics of grouping, in the way of Kanizsa shapes as ‘occluders’, and filling-in of partial images. We ran two behavioural and one EEG experiment.

We recorded EEG data from 30 participants while they looked at a fast-rate stream of the stimuli (Kanizsa shapes with partial images). They were instructed to press a button every time they saw the target (Kanizsa shapes without partial images).
